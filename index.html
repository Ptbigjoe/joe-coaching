Stop and call your midwife or doctor if you notice

  • Bleeding or fluid leaking
  • Dizziness, faintness, or a headache
  • Chest pain or feeling breathless before working hard
  • Painful tightenings or tummy pain
  • Calf pain or swelling
  • Baby moving noticeably less than usual

House rules for these 12 weeks

  • Stay at or under the effort shown on each exercise — if a rep feels like a grind, stop the set.
  • Breathe out on the hard part. Never hold your breath.
  • From week 5 on, nothing flat on your back — the plan already handles this for you.
  • Watch your belly's midline on core and pressing work. If it domes or "cones", swap the exercise (see below).
  • Train cool, sip water often, and stop before exhaustion.
  • Later on, keep a hand on the rack for anything one-legged.

House rules

  • This rule outranks the written program — pain always wins.
  • Trap bar work only enters from Phase 2. It's earned, not skipped ahead.
  • Low pogo hops (weeks 9 & 11) only run if Achilles pain is 2 or under that day. Otherwise swap to isometric calf raise.
  • Sled work is gated behind pain ≤3 on the day.
  • Score shoulder, low back, and knee before and after every session — trends matter more than any single day.
  • Week 8 is a full deload: half the volume, RPE capped at 5, no sled. It's what makes weeks 9–12 the strongest of the block.
